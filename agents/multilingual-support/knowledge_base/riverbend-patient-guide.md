# Riverbend Physical Therapy: Patient Guide (fictional)

Riverbend Physical Therapy is a fictional outpatient physical therapy group with six clinics in the Houston,
Texas area. This guide is the only source the patient support line may answer from.

## Clinics and addresses
- Heights: 1420 Yale Street, Suite 200. Free parking garage behind the building, level 2 connects to the clinic.
- Bellaire: 5110 Bissonnet Street. Free surface lot in front.
- Pasadena: 3305 Spencer Highway. Free surface lot; use the side entrance by the pharmacy.
- Katy: 23510 Kingsland Boulevard, Suite 140. Free surface lot.
- Alief: 11800 Bellaire Boulevard, Suite 15. Free lot shared with the shopping center. Vietnamese-speaking front desk on weekdays.
- Spring: 6920 Louetta Road. Free surface lot.
All clinics are wheelchair accessible.

## Phone numbers
- Main line for every clinic and the front desk: (713) 555-0140.
- Billing staff: (713) 555-0140, option 2, Monday to Friday 8:00 am to 5:00 pm.
- How to say the main number aloud (always exactly ten digits: 7-1-3, 5-5-5, 0-1-4-0):
  - English: "seven one three, five five five, zero one four zero".
  - Spanish: "siete uno tres, cinco cinco cinco, cero uno cuatro cero".
  - Vietnamese: "bảy một ba, năm năm năm, không một bốn không".

## Hours
- All clinics: Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 7:00 pm.
- Saturday: 8:00 am to 1:00 pm at Heights and Katy only. The other four clinics are closed on Saturday.
- All clinics are closed on Sunday.
- Phone support line: Monday to Friday 7:00 am to 7:00 pm. Staff can return messages left after hours the next business day.

## What the support line can and cannot do
- It answers questions from this guide, and can look up a patient's balance, next appointment, clinic and
  therapist after verifying identity with the patient's full name and date of birth.
- Account details are only for the patient. Whoever is calling must give the patient's full name and date of
  birth on this call; there is no separate permission process for family members or employers.
- It cannot see cancellation history or fee waivers (front desk staff can check those and apply the yearly waiver), change or cancel appointments, change therapists, change
  charges, or transfer a call live to a person.
- For anything else it creates one callback request for staff who speak the caller's language. Staff call back
  within one business day. Callback requests are not marked urgent; a caller who needs help sooner can call the
  main line during open hours.

## Visits
- First visit (evaluation): 60 minutes. Follow-up visits: 45 minutes.
- Arrive 15 minutes early for a first visit.
- What to bring: photo ID, insurance card, a list of current medications, any referral or prescription for
  physical therapy, and comfortable clothes you can move in (shorts for knee or hip problems, a loose shirt
  for shoulder or neck problems).

## Insurance
Accepted plans:
- Lone Star Health PPO.
- BayouCare HMO: a referral from your primary care doctor is required before the first visit.
- Gulf Coast Medicare Advantage.
- Medicare (original).
- Workers' compensation: a claim number and the adjuster's contact are required before the first visit.
Riverbend does not accept other plans in network. Patients with other plans can pay as self-pay.
The support line cannot confirm coverage, deductibles or copays for a specific patient. Patients should call
the number on the back of their insurance card, or ask billing staff.

## Self-pay prices
- Evaluation (first visit): $160.
- Follow-up visit: $110.
- Payment plans are available; billing staff set them up.

## Cancellation and no-show policy
- Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the visit.
- A cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice, or a no-show, has a $40 fee.
- The fee is waived once per calendar year per patient.
- Staff cannot waive the fee a second time by phone; requests go to the front desk manager.

## Billing basics
- Statements are mailed and emailed monthly.
- Patients can pay online in the patient portal, by phone with billing staff, or at any front desk.
- Questions about a specific charge, disputes, refunds and payment plans are handled by billing staff, not
  the support line.

## Home exercise portal
- Patients get their home exercise program in the Riverbend patient portal at portal.riverbendpt.example.
- First login: use the email on file and the 6-digit code texted to the phone on file.
- Forgot password: tap "Forgot password" on the login page; a reset link is emailed.
- Exercise videos are narrated (spoken) in English, Spanish and Vietnamese. They have no subtitles. Change the
  narration language under Settings > Language.
- If the code never arrives, the phone number on file may be wrong; front desk staff can update it.

## Safety
- Riverbend never gives medical advice by phone, including on pain, medications or whether to keep exercising.
- Severe new pain, numbness or weakness, chest pain, or trouble breathing: call 911 or go to the nearest
  emergency room now.
- For other questions about symptoms or exercises, patients should ask their therapist at the next visit or
  leave a message for their therapist with staff.
