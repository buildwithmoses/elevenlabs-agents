# Riverbend Physical Therapy (fictional)

Everything here is made up: the clinic, its staff, patients, insurers, prices and videos. Phone numbers use
555-01xx, domains end in `.example`, and no real person or business is described. No real patient data is used.

Riverbend runs **six outpatient physical therapy clinics in the Houston, TX area** (Heights, Bellaire,
Pasadena, Katy, Alief, Spring). 41 therapists. About a third of patients prefer Spanish, and Alief has many
Vietnamese-speaking patients.

- Hours: Mon–Fri 7am–7pm, Sat 8am–1pm (Heights and Katy only). Visits are 45 minutes; evaluations 60.
- Insurance accepted: Lone Star Health PPO, BayouCare HMO (needs a referral), Gulf Coast Medicare Advantage,
  Medicare, workers' comp (needs a claim number). Self-pay: evaluation $160, follow-up $110.
- Cancellation policy: 24 hours' notice; under 24 hours or no-show is a $40 fee (waived once per year).
- Riverbend never gives medical advice by phone. Severe new pain, numbness, chest pain or trouble breathing:
  call 911 or go to an emergency room.

## The problems leadership wants voice AI to fix

1. **No-shows** run 14%. The front desk has no time to call tomorrow's patients, and freed slots stay empty.
2. **New-patient calls** go to voicemail at busy times, and half of those callers book elsewhere.
3. **Spanish and Vietnamese callers** wait for one of three bilingual staff.
4. **Call quality** is reviewed by listening to 5 random calls a month. HIPAA identity checks get skipped.
5. **Home exercise videos** exist only in English, so non-English speakers do their exercises wrong.

## People (approvers)

- **Dr. Elena Ruiz, DPT** (clinical director): approves anything clinical, including translated exercise videos.
- **Marcus Bell** (front desk manager): owns scheduling and the waitlist.
- **Hannah Cho** (compliance): owns the call QA rubric and HIPAA identity checks.

Standing rule: **the voice agent schedules, answers from the knowledge base and hands off. It never gives
medical advice, never reveals health information before verifying identity, and anything clinical waits for
Dr. Ruiz.**
